Comprehensive STEM Programs

Discover our carefully crafted curriculum designed to inspire and challenge students at every educational level.

Elementary School Program (K-6)

Building strong foundations in STEM through interactive learning, hands-on experiments, and age-appropriate challenges. Our elementary program focuses on sparking curiosity and developing problem-solving skills that last a lifetime.

🔬

Science Discovery

Explore the natural world through hands-on experiments, observation, and scientific inquiry.

Difficulty Level Beginner
  • • Simple chemistry experiments
  • • Plant and animal studies
  • • Weather and climate basics
  • • Physics of motion and energy
📊

Mathematical Thinking

Develop logical reasoning and mathematical concepts through games and real-world applications.

Difficulty Level Beginner
  • • Number patterns and sequences
  • • Geometry and spatial reasoning
  • • Data collection and graphs
  • • Problem-solving strategies
💻

Technology Basics

Introduction to coding, robotics, and digital tools through playful exploration.

Difficulty Level Beginner
  • • Block-based programming
  • • Simple robotics projects
  • • Digital citizenship
  • • Creative technology tools

Program Highlights

Learning Outcomes

  • • Develop scientific curiosity and observation skills
  • • Build mathematical confidence and number sense
  • • Learn basic programming logic and computational thinking
  • • Practice collaboration and communication skills
  • • Understand real-world applications of STEM concepts

Program Details

  • • Class Size: Maximum 12 students
  • • Duration: 2 hours per week
  • • Schedule: Weekday afternoons & weekends
  • • Assessment: Portfolio-based evaluation
  • • Materials: All equipment provided

Junior High School Program (7-9)

Intermediate STEM concepts with increased complexity and laboratory work. Students develop analytical thinking, advanced problem-solving skills, and begin exploring specialized areas of interest.

⚛️

Advanced Science

Deep dive into chemistry, physics, and biology with laboratory experiments and data analysis.

Difficulty Level Intermediate
  • • Chemical reactions and stoichiometry
  • • Physics of forces and energy
  • • Cellular biology and genetics
  • • Scientific method and research
🤖

Engineering & Robotics

Design, build, and program robots while learning engineering principles and teamwork.

Difficulty Level Intermediate
  • • Mechanical design and construction
  • • Programming with sensors
  • • Team-based robotics challenges
  • • Engineering design process
📈

Data Science

Learn to collect, analyze, and visualize data using modern tools and statistical methods.

Difficulty Level Intermediate
  • • Statistical analysis and probability
  • • Data visualization techniques
  • • Spreadsheet and database skills
  • • Real-world data projects

Program Highlights

Learning Outcomes

  • • Master intermediate scientific concepts and lab techniques
  • • Develop engineering design and problem-solving skills
  • • Learn data analysis and statistical reasoning
  • • Build teamwork and project management abilities
  • • Prepare for advanced high school coursework

Program Details

  • • Class Size: Maximum 10 students
  • • Duration: 3 hours per week
  • • Schedule: Weekday evenings & weekends
  • • Assessment: Project-based evaluation
  • • Prerequisites: Elementary STEM completion

Senior High School Program (10-12)

Advanced placement preparation, independent research projects, and career-focused learning. Prepare for university success and professional STEM careers.

🧬

Advanced Science

AP-level coursework in biology, chemistry, and physics with independent research projects.

Difficulty Level Advanced
  • • AP Biology, Chemistry, Physics
  • • Independent research methodology
  • • Advanced laboratory techniques
  • • Scientific writing and presentation
💻

Computer Science

Programming, algorithms, and software development with real-world applications.

Difficulty Level Advanced
  • • Java/Python programming
  • • Data structures and algorithms
  • • Web and mobile development
  • • Machine learning basics
🚀

Engineering Design

Capstone projects, engineering principles, and preparation for university engineering programs.

Difficulty Level Advanced
  • • Engineering design process
  • • CAD modeling and 3D printing
  • • Capstone project development
  • • University preparation

Program Highlights

Learning Outcomes

  • • Master advanced STEM concepts and AP-level content
  • • Conduct independent research and complete capstone projects
  • • Develop professional programming and technical skills
  • • Build college-ready portfolio and resume
  • • Prepare for STEM careers and university programs

Program Details

  • • Class Size: Maximum 8 students
  • • Duration: 4 hours per week
  • • Schedule: Flexible evening & weekend options
  • • Assessment: Research projects and AP prep
  • • Prerequisites: Junior High STEM or equivalent
