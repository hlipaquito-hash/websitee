Tentang STEM X Football Academy

Kami menghadirkan pendekatan revolusioner: Bimbingan Belajar STEM melalui dinamika sepak bola! Mata pelajaran abstrak seperti Matematika, Fisika, Biologi, dan Data Analisis diwujudkan dalam project-based learning yang menyenangkan exceptional STEM education and hands-on learning experiences.

Our Mission

Kami menghadirkan kurikulum STEM inovatif yang mengintegrasikan sepak bola dengan pembelajaran berkualitas. Program kami tidak hanya membangun kemampuan berpikir kritis, literasi sains, dan keterampilan matematika, tetapi juga memperdalam pemahaman sepak bola melalui pendekatan sistematis,menciptakan proses belajar yang utuh dan aplikatif. .

Through hands-on learning, expert instruction, and a curriculum grounded in real-world applications, we empower students to develop critical thinking skills, creativity, and confidence in their ability to tackle complex problems through football.

Our Story

Founded in 2018 by a team of passionate educators and industry professionals, STEM Academy has grown from a small after-school program to a leading STEM education provider.

2018

Foundation

STEM Academy was founded with a simple belief: every child deserves access to high-quality STEM education. We started with just 15 students in a small classroom, focusing on hands-on learning and personalized attention.

2020

Growth & Innovation

Despite global challenges, we adapted to virtual learning and expanded our programs. Our student body grew to 150, and we introduced advanced robotics and coding programs.

2023

Excellence Recognized

Our students began winning regional and national competitions. We received the "Outstanding STEM Education Provider" award and expanded to serve over 300 students across three locations.

2025

Future Forward

Today, we continue to innovate with AI integration, advanced laboratories, and industry partnerships. Our alumni are pursuing STEM degrees at top universities around the world.
